Meet the director

Technology should make work simpler.

I’m Samuel Mutua Juma, an ICT professional and the Director of Parlpoint ICT. I work with businesses and institutions to solve everyday technology challenges and develop digital tools that support better operations.

My professional focus is on translating technology into practical results. I support the full technology lifecycle: assessing user and organisational needs, planning solutions, configuring equipment, maintaining systems, resolving incidents, documenting procedures and training users. I hold a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology from Kibabii University, graduating with Second Class Honours – Upper Division.

Institutional & administrative support

Practical digital support for your organisation

We can assist schools and other organisations with routine computer-based administrative tasks, document preparation and digital filing processes, subject to the organisation’s authorised access and approvals.

  • PAYE return preparation and filing support through the relevant official platform
  • SHA and NSSF registration, contribution and portal-process assistance
  • Online applications, document uploads and records organisation
  • Data entry, spreadsheet preparation, typing and report formatting
  • School records, assessment documents, curriculum layouts and schemes of work
  • Staff guidance on email, office applications and safe digital practices

Statutory submissions remain the responsibility of the organisation and its authorised representatives. We provide administrative and technical assistance; we do not act as a government agency, tax agent or statutory authority.
